1. Introduction

Welcome to Kurdo Gift ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is handled.

📊

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you create an account, we may collect the following:

Data Type Purpose Required
Full Name Account identification & display Yes
Email Address Account login & communication Yes
Phone Number Account verification No
Profile Photo Personalization No
Password Account security Yes

2.2 Transaction Data

We collect data related to your transactions within the App, including:

  • Purchase history and order details
  • Account balance and points
  • Raffle and spin wheel participation records
  • Deposit and withdrawal requests

2.3 Device & Technical Data

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push notifications
  • Device type and operating system version

We do NOT collect location data, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, or any biometric data.

⚙️

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To create, maintain, and secure your account
  • Service Delivery: To process purchases, manage balances, and deliver digital gift cards
  • Rewards & Promotions: To operate raffles, spin wheels, and points-based rewards
  • Notifications: To send important updates about your account, prizes, and new features
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and resolve issues
  • App Improvement: To improve the functionality and user experience of the App

We do NOT use your data for targeted advertising, profiling, or selling to advertisers.

🔒

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS (SSL certificates)
  • Password Security: Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • PIN Protection: App lock PINs are encrypted using SHA-256 hashing
  • Secure Token Storage: Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted secure storage on your device
  • Cleartext Prevention: The App blocks all non-HTTPS (cleartext) network traffic
  • Server Security: Our servers are hosted on secure infrastructure with firewall protection

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
